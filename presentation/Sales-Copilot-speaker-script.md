## Slide 1: Title (0:00 to 0:20)

Hi everyone, we are [team name]. We improved the sales CRM of a car dealership, CS Euro Auto. We call it **Sales Copilot**: an AI assistant that tells your sales team what to do next.

## Slide 2: Monday morning (0:20 to 1:10)

Picture a salesperson on Monday at 9 am. They open the CRM, and **91 deals are sitting in "Follow Up", worth about 3.1 million ringgit.** That is the production CRM we studied.

The question in their head is simple: **"Which one do I call first?"** The CRM does not answer it. They have to open each deal, read it, and decide.

## Slide 3: Our idea (1:10 to 1:55)

Our idea: **three steps become one click.**

Before, a salesperson reads the record, decides what to do, and writes the email. That takes minutes per customer, which is our estimate. After, they click **AI Summary**, and it takes under a second, which we measured.

We added it to the existing page. **We removed nothing and changed no workflow.**

## Slide 4: Same CRM, a modern look (1:55 to 2:35)

We also gave the CRM itself a fresh look. **On the left is the original screen, on the right is the same screen after our restyle.** Same data, same features, but clearer and easier to scan.

We did it as a style layer on top, so **no CRM core file was changed.**

## Slide 5: Summary and next action (2:35 to 3:50)

Here it is, in the real CRM. This is an opportunity for a Peugeot 5008, in the Quotation stage.

One click on **AI Summary**, and in under a second we get three things. One: the facts at a glance, the customer, the vehicle, the budget, the stage and the last activity. Two: a plain-language summary. Three: **the recommended next action, with a priority and a reason.**

Here it says: follow up on the quotation today, because it was sent six days ago and the customer has not replied. The salesperson does not need to read the whole record to know what to do.

*Live demo cue (optional, 30 seconds): switch to the CRM tab, click AI Summary, then switch back to the slides.*

## Slide 6: Follow-up email (3:50 to 4:50)

Next, the follow-up email. One more click, and the assistant writes it, using the customer's name, the vehicle, the quoted price and the current stage.

The salesperson can **edit it, copy it, or regenerate a different version.** And importantly, **it never sends anything by itself.** The salesperson stays in control.

*Live demo cue (optional): click Generate Follow-up Email, change one word, click Regenerate.*

## Slide 7: Who to call first (4:50 to 5:30)

We also added a short list on the home dashboard. **Red means urgent**, like a follow-up that is overdue or a quotation with no reply. **Yellow means something is coming up**, like a test drive tomorrow. Click a name, and the deal opens.

## Slide 8: Results and next steps (5:30 to 6:40)

What does this change? Reading, deciding and writing by hand takes **minutes per customer**. That is our estimate. With the assistant, it takes **one click and under a second**, and we measured that in our demo.

I want to be honest about where we are. Today, the assistant **works end to end inside the CRM, using real customer data**, but the wording comes from rules we wrote, not from a language model yet.

Our next steps: connect it to the production data, plug in Claude so the writing is more natural and it can understand the free-text notes, and let staff send the email from the CRM after they approve it.

## Likely questions, with honest answers

**Is this really AI?**
Today the writing is rule-based: it reads the real CRM record and applies logic. It is wired to use Claude when we add an API key, and that is our next step.

**Is that real customer data?**
No. It runs on a SuiteCRM instance with demo customers we created, because we could not use the production data. Connecting the production CRM is a next step.

**What if the AI is wrong?**
It only suggests. Staff review everything, and the email is never sent automatically.

**How did you add it without changing the CRM?**
Through SuiteCRM's standard custom folder. The core CRM files are untouched, so the existing pages and features work exactly as before. The new look is also a style layer that can be switched off.

**What about privacy?**
When Claude is used, we send only the fields it needs. The customer's phone number and email address are not sent.

**How much time does it save?**
The assistant answers in under a second, which we measured. The manual time is our estimate of a few minutes per customer, and we would measure it properly with real staff.

**Why rules first?**
They are instant, predictable and work offline. A language model adds more natural wording and understanding of free text.
